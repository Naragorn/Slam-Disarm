# A real slam: the player grabs the NPC's sword forearm, lifts it up and out to his side, and slams it down onto a block
# twice (BasHarness player-grab-npc, player-slam, place-block: real physics, the game's own collision events). The block
# stands in for a table: a static collider without a ColliderGroup, which the mod counts as World. The second slam
# disarms; the hand may not pick the sword up again for No Regrab Time.
# player-eyes first: the mock headset sits 0.16 m above the creature's eyes otherwise, and the game measures the grab's
# reach from the head (PlayerHand drops a grip farther than 1.22 m from it).
# The lift: his hand hangs in front of his thigh, so a block under it would sit inside his leg, and an arm pulled further
# down from there meets the end of his shoulder's reach (2026-11-23: at most 0.24 m of travel, 2.5 m/s at best). Lifted
# 0.3 m and moved 0.2 m out, the hand falls 0.24 m onto the block and meets it at about 2.6 m/s.
# What this caught (2026-11-23): the game raises no collision events for a standing NPC's hand (its CollisionHandler is
# off; the wrist handle turns them on for the arms only), so the palm hit the block at 2.7 m/s and the mod saw nothing.
# The mod now turns the hand's events on while it watches the grab (Logic/HandEvents).
wait-level
set-option SlamDisarm "Slam Disarm Enabled" true
set-option SlamDisarm "Debug Logging" true
set-option SlamDisarm "Slams To Disarm" 2
set-option SlamDisarm "Min Slam Speed" 2.5
set-option SlamDisarm "Slam Window" 3
set-option SlamDisarm "Disarm Chance" 1
set-option SlamDisarm "No Regrab Time" 5
frame-rate 90
player-eyes

# HumanDummy runs no behaviour tree, so it never draws its weapon: put the sword into its hand.
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=0.5 facing=0 brain=HumanDummy container=CivilianMeleeSword
npc-grab SwordShortCommon hand=left
expect-npc holding True within=2
player-grab-npc LeftArm section=Lower
expect-log [SlamDisarm] GRAB |
wait 1
player-slam 0.2,0.3,0 speed=1.5 hold=1 back=false
place-block 0,-0.30,0 size=0.12 from=npc-hand
record-start slam fps=30 width=960 height=540 views=side,npc distance=1.8 rise=0.8 angle=90
mark
player-slam 0,-0.7,0 speed=6
expect-log [SlamDisarm] SLAM |
wait 0.5
player-slam 0,-0.7,0 speed=6
await-log 2 [SlamDisarm] DISARM |
wait 1
record-stop
expect-npc holding False within=2
player-release

# No regrab: a sword put into the hand is let go again.
mark
npc-grab SwordShortCommon hand=left
expect-npc holding False within=1
expect-log [SlamDisarm] REGRAB |
expect-no-log Exception
quit
